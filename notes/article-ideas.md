# Article ideas (parked)

## Spittlebug foam

**Status:** parked, to be written at length later. Separate article — do not fold into
"Let's talk about poop".

**Core hook:** how does the nymph breathe while sealed inside its own foam?

Angles to chase when writing:

- The breathing question itself — is it a snorkel arrangement, an air store held against
  the body, gas exchange through the foam, or some combination? This is the spine of the
  piece, so it deserves the most digging.
- What the foam is actually made of, and where it comes from — xylem sap is famously
  nutrient-poor, so the volume passing through the animal to make this much froth is a
  story in itself.
- What the foam is *for*: predator concealment, humidity, thermal buffering. Probably
  several jobs at once; worth separating what's demonstrated from what's assumed.
- How the bubbles get made mechanically.
- Why xylem-feeding is such a hard living, and who else attempts it.
- The naming tangle: spittlebug / froghopper / cuckoo spit, and which name goes with the
  nymph vs. the adult.

**Do not publish claims from memory** — every mechanism above needs a source before it
goes on the page.
